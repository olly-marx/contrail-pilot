Contrail Pilot: privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026. This page covers the Contrail Pilot game on the web (olly-marx.github.io/contrail-pilot) and in the iPhone and Android apps.

In short

Contrail Pilot has no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. Your game is saved on your device. If you choose to go online, a small amount of game data is stored so that the leaderboards work and you can bring your pilot back on another device.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game (planes, upgrades, nature, missions and settings), kept in the app's or browser's local storage.
  • Flights that have not been sent yet, while you are offline.

Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes all of it.

What is stored online, and why

When the game connects, it signs in with an anonymous account: no email and no password. It then stores:

  • Your pilot name, if you make one: a first name you type (letters only), a word from a fixed list and a number, for example "Sam the Puffin 42". It shows on the leaderboards.
  • Your pilot code, which lets you bring your pilot back on another device.
  • A backup copy of your saved game.
  • Flight results (distance, landings and similar game numbers) for the leaderboards.
  • The game version you are running.

This is held by our database provider, Supabase, on our behalf. It is used only to run the game. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you. Use a first name or a nickname, never your full name.

Children

The game is made to be safe for children. It collects no contact details and shows no adverts. The only text other players can see is the pilot name, made from a first name, a word from a fixed list and a number.

Removing your data

To remove your online pilot and its saved game, open an issue at github.com/olly-marx/contrail-pilot/issues asking for deletion. Give your pilot name only, never your pilot code. The online copy is then deleted.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
