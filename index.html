About us

Aerospace Environmental Impacts Simulation

This game was made by the Aerospace Environmental Impacts Simulation (AEIS) group, part of the Brahmal Vasudevan Institute for Sustainable Aviation at Imperial College London.

We use computer models and real observations to understand how planes affect the climate and the air we breathe, including how contrails warm or cool the Earth.

The AEIS group >The Brahmal Institute >Real flights dodging contrails >

The trees and rewilding in the game are inspired by Citizen Zoo, who restore wild places and bring back animals in and around London.

Citizen Zoo >

Made for the Great Exhibition Road Festival. The game keeps the science simple: real contrails in sunshine don't always cool the Earth. Ask us at the stand!

How to play

Your job: fly far, keep the Earth cool, and land safely.

  1. Tap to fly. Miss the storms.
  2. Grab seeds. Land at an airport to keep them.
  3. Sunny: fly INTO icy air. Your contrail makes shade. Cooler!
  4. Night: fly AROUND icy air. Contrails trap heat. Hotter! Dodging earns ice crystals.
  5. Hangar: spend seeds and crystals on a cleaner plane and on nature.
