How to play

Your job: fly far, keep the Earth cool, and land safely.

  1. Tap to fly. Miss the storms.
  2. Grab seeds. Land at an airport to keep them.
  3. Sunny: fly INTO icy air. Your contrail makes shade. Cooler!
  4. Night: fly AROUND icy air. Contrails trap heat. Hotter! Dodging earns ice crystals.
  5. Hangar: spend seeds and crystals on a cleaner plane and on nature.
