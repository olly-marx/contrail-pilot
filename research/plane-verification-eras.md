## Suggested replacements
1. **Raked wingtips (1990s twin):** these are 2000s. Replace with "Winglets on the A330/A340 (1993)" or keep "raked tips" only if the era label says 2000s.
2. **First computer forecasts (1970s):** move to 1950s ("first computer forecast, 1955"). For 1970s, use "global forecast models: ECMWF, 1979".
3. **Radar control (1970s):** rename to "Computer radar with altitude readout (1970s)".
4. **Swept fan blades (1990s):** rename to "Wide-chord fan blades". Swept blades are 2000s.
5. **Sharkskin riblets:** label as "riblet test (A320, late 1980s)", or move to 2020s with AeroSHARK.
6. **Lie-flat beds:** say "flat beds in first class (1995)". Business-class beds are 2000.
7. **Comet rounded windows:** reword the effect rationale. The fix was fatigue strength (oval windows plus thicker skin), not altitude.
8. **Ten across:** say "nine or ten across".
9. **Jet A:** say "standard kerosene jet fuel (late 1950s)".
