Contrail Pilot: the five songs

Made in this page with Web Audio (no sound files, works offline); the code is the game's own music code. Each song plays its LONG form once (about 80-90 seconds): A, then A again a little higher, then a new B part, B higher, then A twice more. In the game each song repeats a few times, then the next one starts. Nobody on the team has heard these: they are drafts for your ears.

Playlist

The five songs in a random order, each once (a shuffle: the same song never plays twice in a row), with a one second crossfade. In the game each song plays three times before the next.

Duck test

While a song plays, press this: a short bright reward sound plays and the music dips under it for about 1.5 seconds, then comes back.
