Contrail Pilot: help

Contrail Pilot is a retro flying game about contrails and climate. You fly between real cities, keep out of the icy air that makes warming contrails, and plant trees and water life to bring each land's nature back.

Questions

How do I fly?
Tap, or press the space bar, to climb. Let go to glide down. Land on the runway before the yellow line.
Why does icy air matter?
Planes flying through cold, wet air leave contrails that stay in the sky and trap heat, mostly at night. Climbing or diving out of that air is a real way pilots can cut warming, and the game rewards it.
How do I keep my game on a new phone?
Make a pilot on the PILOT screen and note your pilot code. On the new device, choose "I already have a pilot" and enter the code.
I missed the airport on a story. What happens?
The big "LAND AT" call shows as you get close. If you fly past the stop and land somewhere else, you lose a heart (Easy stories have no hearts) and the stop stays your next one.
Does the game need the internet?
No. You can play offline; leaderboards and backups catch up when you are back online.

Report a problem

Testing the app? Use TestFlight's "Send Beta Feedback" (take a screenshot in the app). Otherwise, open an issue at github.com/olly-marx/contrail-pilot/issues, saying what you did and which device you were on. Never post your pilot code.
